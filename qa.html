General
What is Pawpaw Ko?

Pawpaw Ko is a community-first pop-up gaming company that runs trading card game and board game events at local businesses and community centers across NYC. We also host a Trades hub so players can find local trade partners.

Do I need an account to browse binders?

No. Anyone can browse public binders without signing in. You'll need an account only to build your own binder, list cards, or contact other traders.

Trades
How do trades actually happen?

Trades are coordinated out-of-band. Each binder lists the owner's Discord handle — message them to set up a meet at a local shop, event, or wherever's convenient. We don't handle payment or shipping.

What do the labels mean — trade, sell, free, combo?

Trade: open to swapping for other cards. Sell: looking for cash (Venmo or in-person, your call). Free: giving it away. Combo: bundled with other cards as a set.

Can I have more than one binder?

Yes — create as many as you'd like from My Binders.

Events
Where are events held?

At rotating local partners — cafés, community centers, and shops across the five boroughs. Follow @pawpaw_plays for upcoming dates and locations.

Do I need to bring my own cards?

For TCG events, yes — bring your deck. For board game nights and intro events, we provide everything.
