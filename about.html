About Us

Our mission, our community, our table

Who We Are

Pawpaw Ko is a community-first pop-up gaming company dedicated to bringing trading card games and board games directly to the spaces where people already gather. We work with local businesses, community centers, and volunteer sites to create accessible, welcoming gaming experiences. Our mission is to strengthen the bonds within the gaming community while contributing meaningfully to the broader neighborhoods we call home.

We believe the table is one of the most powerful places for community and connection. By partnering with local businesses and organizations, we keep dollars in the community, support causes greater than ourselves, and introduce the joy of tabletop gaming to people who haven't found their way into the hobby yet.

Gather at the table. Grow in the community.
